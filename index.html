We organize this online research seminar series in finance and accounting to facilitate the exchange of ideas between academics and practitioners and to bring leading academic research to a broader audience.

The seminar has been initiated by Peter Limbach (University of Bielefeld and CFR) and is organized by him as well as Gerrit Köchling (Ilmenau University of Technology). We thank Christian Andres and Nic Schaub (WHU), André Betzer (University of Wuppertal), Hendrik Hakenes (University of Bonn), Peter N. Posch (TU Dortmund University), the Hamburg Financial Research Center, and colleagues from various European and U.S. universities for their support.

We are proud to present internationally recognized academic speakers and kindly invite you to join the discussion on Zoom (please join the mailing list below).

If you have any questions, send an email to info@jointfinanceseminar.com.
