Who is responsible

Gerrit Köchling is responsible for the processing of personal data described here (contact above).

Mailing list

If you subscribe, we store your email address to send you the line-up of each round and reminders before each talk. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email, or by writing to us. We keep your address until you unsubscribe.

The list runs on Mailchimp (Intuit Inc., USA), which processes the data on our behalf, possibly also in the USA. Mailchimp records when you subscribed and may record whether you open our emails and which links you click. Details: Mailchimp privacy statement.

Seminar sessions on Zoom

The talks take place on Zoom, through Bielefeld University's Zoom account. When you join, Zoom processes the name you enter and technical data of your connection. During the session we save Zoom's automatic captions and the chat. We use them only to send the speaker an overview of the questions and comments from the audience, including the names of those who made them. We do not publish the captions or the chat.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When a page is visited, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address and stores it for security purposes. We set no cookies and use no analytics; the font is stored on this website. Details: GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, write to info@jointfinanceseminar.com.
