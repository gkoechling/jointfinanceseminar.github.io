Free data sources

Freely available data that researchers in finance and accounting use, collected by the organizers. We opened and checked every link; most files can be downloaded directly, a few need a free registration. The list is not complete, and terms of use differ, so please check each provider's conditions before using the data.

Euro Area Monetary Policy Event-Study Database (EA-MPD)
Carlo Altavilla and coauthors
Intraday changes in OIS rates, euro area sovereign yields, stock indices, and euro exchange rates around ECB press releases and press conferences, 1999–2025.
Eurostat database
Eurostat (European Commission)
Official EU statistics as multi-dimensional datasets, with bulk downloads.
FRED
Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis
Hundreds of thousands of economic and financial time series; download via website, API (free key), or Excel add-in.
FRED-MD and FRED-QD
Michael W. McCracken (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis)
Large monthly (FRED-MD) and quarterly (FRED-QD) macroeconomic databases built from FRED, with monthly vintages and historical vintages from 1999 (MD) and 2018 (QD).
Global Capital Allocation Project datafree for non-commercial academic use
Global Capital Allocation Project
Restated bilateral external portfolios, security-level U.S. fund holdings, currency usage series, and replication data on capital allocation by country, currency, and asset class.
Global Macro Database
Karsten Müller and coauthors
46 macroeconomic variables for 239 countries from 167 sources, with long historical coverage; free for academic use.
IMF Data
International Monetary Fund
World Economic Outlook, International Financial Statistics, balance of payments, reserve composition (COFER), and prices.
Macrohistory Database

Annual macro-financial data for 18 advanced economies since 1870, including total returns on equities, housing, government bonds, and bills.

Monetary policy and interest-rate series, e.g., monetary policy surprises, market-based policy uncertainty, a proxy funds rate, and an event-study database.

OECD

Our World in Data

Worldwide Governance Indicators
World Bank
